\u27f3 Period / Cycle Reset
\n \u25bc\n
\n
\n
Use these at the end of a business cycle. Each reset automatically exports an Excel archive first so your historical data is always saved before anything is cleared.

Full Cycle Reset
\n
Clears all sales, expenses, returns, cash records & notes.
Keeps: products, stock levels, categories, settings, discount reasons.
\n
\n \n
\n\n \n
\n
\n
Clear Returns Log
\n
Removes all return records from the database.
Use to clear test/accidental returns. Does not affect sales or stock.
\n
\n \n
\n\n \n
\n
\n
Stock Reset
\n
Sets all product stock quantities to zero.
Keeps: all sales history, settings and everything else intact.
\n
\n \n
\n\n \n
\n
\n
Monthly Reset
\n
Clears only this month's sales, expenses & returns.
All other months' history is preserved. Use at month-end.
